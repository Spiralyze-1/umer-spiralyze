INFORMATION

Know before you go

What should I wear?

Business casual

When should I arrive?

Registration opens at 9:00 AM with breakfast and coffee; the program begins promptly at 9:30 AM. We'd recommend arriving by 9:15.

Can I bring a colleague?

All firms receive 2 tickets, please register via the form on this page for all parties who would like to attend.

Will materials be shared afterward?

Yes, recordings or sessions will be shared on the event website.

Is lunch provided? Can dietary requirements be accommodated?

Breakfast, lunch, and an evening reception are all included. All dietary requirements captured during registration will be honored in all provided meals.

What's the Wi-Fi situation?

Wifi will be available all day throughout the event.

Is there a private area I can take a call?

Yes, there are private and semi- private spaces available for meetings and phone calls throughout the day.
