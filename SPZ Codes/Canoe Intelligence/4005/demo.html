Frequently asked questions.

What is Canoe Intelligence and what does it do?

Canoe Intelligence is an alternative investment data automation platform. It automatically collects LP and fund documents from 3,000+ GP and administrator portals, extracts structured data using AI trained exclusively on alternative investments, and delivers clean, standardized data to downstream portfolio management and reporting systems. The platform supports $11T+ in assets under administration across 44,000+ ingested funds with 200M+ data points extracted.
